An industrial plant is described by many kinds of documents: piping and instrumentation diagrams (P&IDs) show how the equipment is connected, vendor datasheets say what each device can do. Engineering questions often need both, for example the allowed pressure of the valve after a pump. Large language models answer such questions reliably only if the structure of the documents (which pipe leads where, which value holds under which condition) is kept. A knowledge graph, a database that stores these relations, can keep it.

The work has two parts. The drawing part builds a knowledge graph from all sheets of a plant, reconnecting the lines cut at sheet borders, and answers over it with a two-stage system (a database query first, then a model that calls graph-walking tools if needed). The datasheet part turns one device's PDF datasheet into a graph in which every value keeps its unit, its validity condition and its source page. In this thesis we connect the two: the drawing items are linked to the datasheet graphs by their identifiers (tags) in one database, and we compare three question-answering methods: a system over the merged graph (M1), a router that calls the two existing systems separately (M2), and a graph-free baseline (M3).

We developed the methods on a four-sheet plant and then measured them unchanged on a 27-sheet synthetic plant with 190 tagged items, on 60 questions and 12 questions that compare several datasheets, with two models. The router with GLM-5.3 Flash was best (51 of 60 correct), significantly ahead of the merged-graph method (42) and the graph-free baseline (35). The two graph-based methods fully solved half of the multi-datasheet questions, the graph-free baseline none. The graph-free method, competitive on the small plant, often wrongly answered that the documents do not contain the answer on the large drawing, and its cost grew with the drawing. The results hold for synthetic plants and model-written reference answers.

Keywords: knowledge graph, GraphRAG, large language models, P&ID, technical datasheets, question answering, industrial documentation.
